Background & Academic Story

My Journey in Computing

Hello! My name is Christian Ikechukwu Madu. I am a 100-level undergraduate student pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Cybersecurity at MIVA Open University, living in Lagos State, Nigeria.

I have always been curious about how computers communicate and how the internet works. Growing up in Lagos, where mobile apps, fintech, and digital services are part of daily life, I noticed how important online security has become for everyday people and businesses. This sparked my interest in studying cybersecurity to understand both how systems are built and how to protect them against unauthorized access.

As a beginner in web development, I am taking CSC 106: Introduction to Web Technologies this semester. Building this portfolio website from scratch has given me hands-on practice with semantic HTML5 elements, CSS3 styling with flexbox and grid, and client-side JavaScript for DOM interaction. I really enjoy seeing my code come to life in the browser without relying on complicated frameworks.

Along with web technologies, I am taking courses in algorithm design and problem-solving (COS 102), calculus (MTH 102), physics (PHY 102 & PHY 108), and technical certifications (MIVA-COS 111). I am focused on building strong academic habits, writing clean code, and preparing myself for a meaningful career in technology.
