How the Study Planner Works

Concept: A student planner built with pure vanilla JavaScript that doesn't need a backend server to store your study tasks.

JavaScript Implementation:

  1. Adding Tasks: Validates task title and captures course code and submission deadline before inserting into the task array.
  2. LocalStorage: Saves the array as a JSON string using localStorage.setItem() so tasks remain even after closing the browser.
  3. State Updates: Clicking the checkbox toggles the task completion status and recalculates the completion rate tally in real time.
  4. Task Deletion: Clicking the delete button removes the item from the array, re-renders the list, and updates local storage.

Nigerian 5-Point University Grading System

Universities in Nigeria use a 5-point Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) system to assess academic standing.

Score Range Letter Grade Grade Point Class of Degree
70% – 100% A 5.0 First Class (4.50 – 5.00)
60% – 69% B 4.0 Second Class Upper (3.50 – 4.49)
50% – 59% C 3.0 Second Class Lower (2.40 – 3.49)
45% – 49% D 2.0 Third Class (1.50 – 2.39)
40% – 44% E 1.0 Pass Degree (1.00 – 1.49)
0% – 39% F 0.0 Fail (Below 1.00)

Formula: GPA = Total Quality Points (Units × Grade Point) ÷ Total Registered Units.

Student Cyber Safety & Hygiene Guide

Practical tips written for university students to stay safe online and prevent account takeovers.

1. Use 3-to-4 Random Words for Passwords

Instead of short passwords like P@ssw0rd1 that can be cracked in minutes, use long passphrases like mango-bicycle-lagos-rain. They are easy to remember and extremely difficult for attackers to guess.

2. Always Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Turn on 2FA on your student portal, personal email, and WhatsApp. An authenticator app (like Google Authenticator) ensures that even if someone learns your password, they cannot access your account.

3. Spotting Phishing Messages & Fake Links

Beware of urgent emails claiming your student portal account is expiring or that you won a scholarship. Always check the sender's actual email address and never enter your password on links sent over SMS or WhatsApp.

4. Caution on Public & Campus Wi-Fi

When connected to public Wi-Fi networks in cafes or campuses, avoid logging into sensitive banking or payment accounts without a VPN, and always verify that the browser URL starts with https://.
